Family Rosary

The Rosary, the Stations and novenas, prayed in your family's own voices.

Each person in your family records the prayers once: the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Glory Be and the rest. Family Rosary then plays them back in order, so you can pray the whole Rosary led by Grandma, Dad and the kids, wherever each of you is.

  • Your family's voices take turns, by decade, by bead, as call and response, or with one person leading the whole Rosary. All four sets of mysteries, with today's mysteries chosen for you.
  • Simple mode: one clear screen with large text, a big Play button and who is leading each prayer, for grandparents and anyone who prefers fewer choices.
  • The Stations of the Cross and novenas, including nine-day novenas the family prays together, with an intention and a check-off for each day.
  • The liturgical calendar: the season and feast days, with the mysteries of the day.
  • Reminders for a daily Rosary and for each day of a family novena.
  • An intentions board where your family shares what to pray for.

Your family circle is private: people join by invitation or with your family's code, and a manager approves each request. You can add a profile for a little one or a grandparent without a phone, and belong to more than one family. No ads, no tracking.
