Family Rosary Support

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Need help with Family Rosary? Email us at support@cipher.haus. We read every message.

In the app, most settings are in My Account & Families: open the Family Circle tab and tap the account button at the top.

Contact us

Write to support@cipher.haus about:

  • trouble signing in or joining a family;
  • a bug, or a recording that won't play or upload: tell us your phone or browser and what you were doing when it happened;
  • privacy questions and data requests, or deleting your account;
  • anything that worries you about a child's safety (put "Child safety" in the subject; see our Child Safety Standards).

If it's about your account, email us from the address you signed up with. We'll never ask for your password, so please don't send it.

Signing in and forgotten passwords

You sign in to Family Rosary with your email address and password, in the iPhone or Android app or in the web app. The same account works everywhere. New passwords need at least 10 characters.

If you've forgotten your password:

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter your account's email address. If an account exists for that address, we email you a link to reset the password.
  3. Open the link and choose a new password, then sign in with it.

If nothing arrives, check your spam folder. While you're signed in, you can also change your password, or send yourself a reset email, in My Account & Families, under Password & Security.

After you sign up, we email you a link to confirm your address. Family invitations sent to your email address appear in the app once you've confirmed it.

Joining a family

There are two ways in:

  • With the family code. Ask someone in the family for its invite code. In the app, choose Join Existing Family (or, if you already belong to a family, tap Join under My Family Circles in My Account & Families), enter the code, your name and your role, and tap Ask to Join. A manager of that family has to approve it before you can see anything in the family. Until then the app shows that your request is waiting.
  • By invitation. A manager can invite you by email from the Family Circle tab. The invitation appears in the app when you sign in with that email address (once you've confirmed it), and you can accept or decline it.

Managers: requests to join appear on the Family Circle tab and in My Account & Families. They came from someone who used your family code, so only approve people you know. We also email you about new requests, and the phone apps can notify you; turn either off in My Account & Families, under Notifications. If the code has reached someone it shouldn't have, tap Make a new code on the Family Circle tab: the old code stops working straight away and everyone already in the family stays in it.

You can belong to more than one family and switch between them in My Account & Families.

Recording prayers

Record in the Studio tab. Each person records each prayer once, and the app uses that recording every time the prayer comes round; for example, one Hail Mary covers every bead. You can also record for a family member without their own account, such as a young child or a grandparent, with their permission (for a child, as their parent or guardian or with that person's permission).

For a voice to lead a whole prayer on its own, it needs:

  • 13 recordings for one Rosary: the Sign of the Cross, the Apostles' Creed, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Glory Be, the Fatima Prayer, the five mysteries being prayed, the Hail, Holy Queen and the closing prayer;
  • 28 recordings to cover all four sets of mysteries;
  • 35 recordings for the Stations of the Cross.

To see what someone still needs, open the Family Circle tab and tap their name. It shows how many prayers they've recorded for today's Rosary, all four sets, the Stations and the current novena, and Record these takes you through the missing ones one at a time.

How the voices take turns (by decade, a new voice for every prayer, a different voice on each Hail Mary bead, or one person leading) and call and response are set for the family in the Prayer Rotation Settings on the Family Circle tab.

If the app can't use the microphone, allow it for Family Rosary in your phone's or browser's settings. The app only uses the microphone while you're recording.

Your recordings follow you

Recordings of your own voice, made on your own profile, are copied to every family you belong to, so you only record them once. Your account also keeps its own private copy of the newest recording of each prayer, so your recordings aren't lost if you leave a family or a family is deleted.

  • My Recordings, in My Account & Families, lists the prayers recorded in your voice, which families use them, and lets you play or delete them, or copy them to a family that doesn't have them.
  • When you create or join a family, the app asks before copying your recordings into it.
  • To stop sharing your recordings with one family, turn off Use my recordings from my account and other families on your own profile in that family.
  • When you delete a recording of your own voice, you choose whether it goes from all your families or only one.

Recordings someone else makes for you stay in the family where they were made.

Simple mode

Simple mode opens the app on one large, calm screen for today's Rosary, with big Play, Back and Next buttons, large text and who is leading each prayer. It's made for grandparents and anyone who prefers fewer choices.

Turn it on in My Account & Families, with Simple mode (large buttons). It's set on each device separately. To leave it, tap Exit simple mode on the simple screen.

Reminders

Reminders work in the iPhone and Android apps, not in the web app. In My Account & Families, under Prayer reminders, you can turn on:

  • a Daily Rosary reminder, at the time and on the days you choose;
  • a Novena reminder for each of the nine days of a novena your family is praying together.

Reminders are set on that phone only and send nothing to us. If one doesn't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Family Rosary in your phone's settings (the app offers a button to open them) and that Do Not Disturb or Focus isn't holding it back.

Reporting a problem

In the app, open My Account & Families and tap Report a problem, under Privacy & Legal. Tell us what went wrong. Unless you untick it, the report also includes technical details (the app version, your device type and the app's recent steps, but no names, emails or recordings) that help us find the cause. Please don't include passwords or other private information. You can also email us.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, in My Account & Families, under Account Actions, or by email if you can't use the app. You choose whether the recordings of your own voice are deleted too or stay with your family. See Delete your account for the steps, and for what's deleted and what stays with your family.

Privacy and data requests

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email support@cipher.haus. See our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a security issue

Please email security@cipher.haus rather than support. Our security.txt has the details.
