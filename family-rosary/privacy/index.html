Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

Family Rosary (the "App") is operated by Cipher Haus, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at support@cipher.haus or by post at Cipher Haus, LLC, 2958 S Victoria Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90016, United States.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools in the App.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and display name, and your password, which Google Firebase Authentication stores as a secure hash.
  • Family circles: each circle's name and invite code, the names, roles and colors of its members (including profiles a family adds for someone without their own account, such as a young child), which accounts belong to it, and who manages it.
  • Requests to join: someone who has a circle's code can ask to join it. A family manager must approve the request before that person can see anything in the circle. When someone asks, we let the circle's managers know by email (unless they turn these emails off) and, on phones where they turned them on, with a notification. The email and notification show the requester's name and the role they entered, and the email also shows their email address if they have confirmed it.
  • Notification settings: whether you want emails about requests to join, and, for each phone on which a manager turns on notifications about requests to join, a notification token for that phone (issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging), whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone, and when it was last updated. We use these only to deliver those notifications. A phone's token is removed when you turn its notifications off, sign out on it, or delete your account.
  • Invitations: the email address, name and role a manager enters to invite someone, and whether it was accepted. When a manager invites someone by email, we send that person an email on the manager's behalf. Invitations are kept until they are accepted, declined or deleted.
  • Family novenas: when someone in a circle starts a novena for the family, we store which novena it is, the day it starts, who started it, the intention they enter (if any), and, for each of the nine days, which family members' profiles were marked as having prayed and by whom. Ended novenas stay with the circle until the circle is deleted.
  • Family intentions: when someone in a circle adds an intention to the family's intentions board, we store its text, who it is for (if they enter a name), the date to pray until (if they choose one), who added it, when, whether it is open, answered or archived, and which members' accounts marked "prayed for this". An intention stays with the circle until its author or a family manager deletes it, or the circle is deleted.
  • Voice recordings: the prayers you, or someone recording on behalf of a family member, record in the Studio, with their length and volume settings. Recordings of your own voice that you make on your own profile are copied to each family circle you belong to, so you don't have to record them again; each circle keeps its own copy. You can turn this off for any circle on your profile there. Recordings someone else makes for you stay in the circle where they were made.
  • Your recordings on your account: your account also keeps a private copy of the newest recording of your own voice for each prayer, so your recordings aren't lost when you leave a circle or a circle is deleted. Only you can see or play this copy. When you create or join a circle, the App asks before copying your recordings into it.
  • On your device: settings, your place in a novena, and any recordings you choose to download for offline use. These stay on your device until you remove them or sign out. The App stores your sign-in state and settings in your browser's or device's storage. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

The App uses the microphone only while you are recording in the Studio. It never records in the background.

If you turn on prayer reminders in the iPhone or Android app, it asks permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and nothing about them is sent to us. If you manage a circle and turn on phone notifications about requests to join, the App asks the same permission and registers that phone as described above. If notifications are already allowed for the App, a manager's phone is registered without asking again; you can turn this off in My Account → Notifications.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your family circles in step across your devices.
  • To play your family's recordings in order during the Rosary, the Stations of the Cross and novenas.
  • To let managers invite people, approve requests to join, and manage their circle, and to tell managers when someone asks to join.

Voice recordings are used only to play them back to your family circle. We don't use them to identify people by their voice, to create voiceprints, or to train any artificial-intelligence model.

Who can see it

  • Your family circle: members of a circle can see its members, recordings (including the copies of your own recordings from your other circles) and invite code, and play its recordings. They can also see the circle's novenas, including their intentions and who prayed each day, and the intentions on the family's intentions board, with who added each one and who prayed for it, so please only write an intention you are happy for your whole family to read. Managers can also see invitations sent for the circle.
  • Google: we store everything with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Hosting), which processes it for us under its terms. Notifications about requests to join are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and on iPhones through Apple's push notification service. The web version of the App also loads fonts and rendering code from Google's servers (gstatic.com), which receive your IP address.
  • Sentry: when the App crashes or hits an error, it sends a diagnostic report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which processes it for us. A report contains the error, the App version, your device or browser type and operating system, the App's state (such as which step of joining or uploading failed) and the steps that led to it. It identifies your account and your family circles only by pseudonymous codes made from their identifiers, so we can tell that several reports came from the same account; it doesn't include your name, email address, recordings or the names of your family circles, and we've configured Sentry not to store IP addresses. If you use Report a problem in My Account, the report also includes what you type, and the technical details above unless you untick them. We use these reports only to fix problems.
  • Cloudflare: a small service we run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) sends invitation emails. It receives the invitee's email address and name, the family's name and the inviter's name, and keeps short-lived counters to limit how many invitations can be sent.
  • Resend: Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) delivers invitation emails for us, from invitations@cipher.haus, and emails to family managers about requests to join, from notifications@cipher.haus. It receives the recipient's email address and the email's contents.
  • Anyone you share a link with: each recording has a private web address. Someone who obtains that exact address can play the recording, so please don't share those addresses outside your family.
  • When the law requires it: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Families may add a profile for a younger child and record prayers in that child's voice only as the child's parent or legal guardian, or with their permission. Those profiles and recordings are visible only within the family circle.

A parent or guardian can remove a child's profile and recordings at any time in the App (a family manager can remove any profile), or by emailing support@cipher.haus. If we learn that we hold an account belonging to a child under 13, we will delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account and family circles exist. Invitations are kept until they are accepted, declined or deleted. To limit how many emails and notifications are sent about requests to join, we keep counters that hold no names or addresses for a few days. When you delete a recording of your own voice, you choose whether it goes from all your circles or only from the one you are in. When you leave a circle, its copy of your recordings stays with it as described below, nothing new is copied to it, and your account keeps its own copy. If you delete a recording from all your circles, it is also removed from your account's copy; deleting it from only one circle leaves your account's copy as it is. When a recording, profile or circle is deleted, or a manager removes someone, we delete the related records and audio files from our systems straight away. Google may hold residual copies for a limited period under its own deletion practices.

Deleting your account

In the App, open My Account & Families → Delete Account, and re-enter your password to confirm. When you delete your account:

  • Recordings of your own voice: you choose whether to delete them too. If you delete them, we remove them, and each circle's copy of them, from every family circle. If you keep them, they stay with your family under your name, as a profile without an account; a family manager can remove that profile at any time. Either way, the copy kept on your account is deleted with your account.
  • Recordings you made for other family members (for example a child or grandparent) always stay with the family.
  • Family novenas you started, and the days marked as prayed, stay with the family, like recordings you made for others.
  • Family intentions you added, and your "prayed for this" marks, stay with the family; you can delete your intentions before you delete your account, and a family manager can delete them at any time.
  • Circles where you were the only member are deleted, with everything in them.
  • Invitations sent to your email are deleted.
  • Phones registered for notifications on your account are removed.
  • Your sign-in account and profile are always deleted.

If you can't use the App, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Our access rules let only members of a circle read its data, and stop members from changing or deleting other people's recordings. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password. Tell us at security@cipher.haus if you find a problem.
