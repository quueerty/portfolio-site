Next Step Pricing

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Next Step is free for clients. Clients never pay, and see nothing about plans or prices, apart from whether a new connection can be made right now.

Clinicians use the clinician portal for free with 1 connected client. A paid plan lets you connect with more clients. Everything else in the portal is the same on every plan. Nobody is limited before January 1, 2027.

Plans for clinicians

A plan of your own covers the clients you see on your own.

  • Free: 1 client.
  • Starter: up to 5 clients. $8.99 a month, or $6.99 a month billed yearly ($83.88 a year).
  • Basic: up to 15 clients. $19.99 a month, or $14.99 a month billed yearly ($179.88 a year).
  • Growth: up to 30 clients. $29.99 a month, or $23.99 a month billed yearly ($287.88 a year).
  • Professional: up to 65 clients. $49.99 a month, or $39.99 a month billed yearly ($479.88 a year).

Plans for practices

A practice pays one price for its plan, however many clinicians it has. The plan's client limit is shared by the clinicians who see clients through the practice, and the practice's owners can set a lower limit for each clinician. A practice can choose any of the paid plans above, or:

  • Clinic (practices only): up to 120 clients. $89.99 a month, or $69.99 a month billed yearly ($839.88 a year).

Need more than 120 clients? Email support@cipher.haus for a custom plan, agreed and invoiced separately.

How plans work

  • Prices are in US dollars and include any applicable taxes.
  • You subscribe in the clinician portal and pay on Stripe's own pages. We never see your card.
  • Plans renew automatically each month or year until you cancel. Stripe emails you a reminder about a week before each renewal.
  • Cancel any time in "Manage billing" on the portal's Billing page. Your plan continues to the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Switching plans takes effect straight away. Within the same billing period, a more expensive plan is charged at once for the rest of the period, and a cheaper one leaves a credit toward your next payments. Switching between monthly and yearly starts a new billing period that day: you're charged the new price at once, less a credit for the unused part of your current period.
  • A limit stops new connections, and stops a connected client being moved into a list that's full (a practice's or your own). Clients already connected stay connected, even if a plan ends or you're over a limit.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and your plan stays in place meanwhile; if every attempt fails, the plan ends.
  • Payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise or where we charged you by mistake.
  • We'll email subscribers at least 30 days before a price change.

The full details are in our Terms of Service, and how we handle billing data is in our Privacy Policy.
