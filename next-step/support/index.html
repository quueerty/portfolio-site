Next Step Support

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Need help with Next Step? Email us at support@cipher.haus. We read every message.

If you need help right now

Next Step isn't an emergency service, and it isn't medical advice or treatment. Our inbox and in-app messages aren't watched around the clock. If you're in danger or need urgent medical help, call your local emergency number now: 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, or 000 in Australia. You can also go to your nearest emergency department.

These are the helplines listed in the app. They're there to listen and support you; for a medical emergency, call your emergency number. Some are open at set hours, so check the line's website for its hours.

United States

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, any time. Free, confidential support for individuals in emotional distress or suicidal crisis. 988lifeline.org
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741, any time. Connect with a trained crisis counselor via text for immediate de-escalation. crisistextline.org
  • ANAD Eating Disorder Helpline: call 1-888-375-7767. Not open 24 hours; see its website for hours. Free, compassionate peer support for eating disorders. anad.org
  • The Trevor Project: call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678, any time. 24/7 crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ youth. thetrevorproject.org

Canada

  • NEDIC (National Eating Disorder Information Centre): call 1-866-633-4220. Not open 24 hours; see its website for hours. Canadian national eating disorder support, guidance, and resources. nedic.ca
  • 9-8-8: Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 9-8-8, any time. National 24/7 bilingual suicide crisis helpline across Canada. 988.ca

United Kingdom

  • Beat Eating Disorders Helpline (England): call 0808 801 0677. Not open 24 hours; see its website for hours. UK eating disorder charity helpline. This number is for England; Beat's website lists its numbers for Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. beateatingdisorders.org.uk
  • Samaritans UK: call 116 123, any time. Free, 24/7 listening service across the UK for anyone in crisis. samaritans.org

Australia

  • Butterfly Foundation National Helpline: call 1800 33 4673. Not open 24 hours; see its website for hours. Free, confidential support for eating disorders and body image distress in Australia. butterfly.org.au
  • Lifeline Australia: call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14, any time. 24/7 crisis support and suicide prevention across Australia. lifeline.org.au

In the app: tap SOS at the top of the main screens. The emergency numbers (911, 999 and 000) are at the top. For helplines, tap 24/7 Helplines and choose your country. The list opens on the United States each time. The Call and Text buttons open your phone's own phone and messages apps. On a tablet or iPad, or if a call or text doesn't start, the app shows the number to use from another phone and, for a helpline, its website.

Contact us

Write to support@cipher.haus about:

  • trouble signing in, with your password, or with two-factor authentication;
  • a bug or crash: tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it happened (this helps most if you've turned crash reporting off);
  • Cloud Backup or syncing problems;
  • a concern about a clinician or a message;
  • privacy questions and data requests, or deleting your account.

If it's about your account, email us from the address you signed up with, and we'll reply to that address to check it's really you before changing anything. If your account's email address was never confirmed, an email from that address doesn't show the account is yours, so we may not be able to act on it. We'll never ask for your password or your two-factor codes, so please don't send them.

In the app, Help is the question-mark button next to SOS. During a check-in, or at the very largest text sizes, Help can be hidden on some screens when there isn't room for it. Settings is the gear button on Home.

Forgotten your password

We only send a reset link to an email address you've confirmed (see Confirming your email address). If you're still signed in on a phone or in the clinician portal, check your Profile before you sign out. In the app, open Settings, then Cloud Backup, then Profile. It shows ✓ Email verified when your address is confirmed. If it asks you to confirm your email instead, do that first. Signing out signs you out on every device, including the clinician portal, and you can only confirm while you're signed in.

  1. Open Settings, then Cloud Backup, then I already have an account. (If you're still signed in on this phone, confirm your email first if Profile asks you to, then tap Sign Out.) If Cloud Backup says "Sign in again to keep backing up", the sign-in form is already open.
  2. Type your email address and tap Forgot password?
  3. Look for an email called "Reset your password — Next Step". The link works for 1 hour.
  4. The link opens a web page where you choose a new password (at least 8 characters).

If your address was never confirmed, you get an email called "About your password reset request — Next Step" instead, with no link. It explains how to confirm your address in the app or the clinician portal where you're still signed in. Then ask for a reset again. If you aren't signed in anywhere, email us. We can't currently reset the password of an account whose email address was never confirmed, because an email from that address doesn't show the account is yours. The data on your phone isn't affected.

The app shows the same message whether or not an account exists for that address, and whether or not the address is confirmed. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder. Resetting your password signs you out on every device, so sign in again on each phone.

Confirming your email address

When you create a Cloud Backup account, we email you a code in an email called "Your confirmation code — Next Step". If you change your email address later, the new address starts unconfirmed and gets a code of its own. Your account works without confirming, but we only send a password reset link to a confirmed address.

To confirm your address, enter the code while you're signed in to your account:

  • in the app: open Settings, then Cloud Backup, then Profile, and look under Account Details; or use the Cloud Backup card on the Backup tab;
  • in the clinician portal: on the dashboard, or on your Profile page.

If you don't have a code, for example because you made your account before our October 2026 update, or your code is more than a day old, choose Send a new code first and use the code from that email. Type the code and choose Confirm. A code expires after 24 hours, and a new code replaces any earlier one. A code only works in the account it was sent for, and only for the address it was sent to.

Opening a link from a confirmation email sent before our October 2026 update doesn't confirm anything now. An address you already confirmed that way stays confirmed: Profile shows ✓ Email verified.

Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is optional. To turn it on, open Cloud Backup and tap Set Up Two-Factor Authentication, then scan the QR code with an authenticator app or enter the key by hand. Keep a copy of that key somewhere safe.

There's currently no built-in way back into your account if you lose your authenticator. The data on your phone isn't affected. If this happens, email us.

Cloud Backup and syncing

  • Cloud Backup is optional. Without it, everything stays on your phone and works offline.
  • It syncs after you make changes and when you tap Sync Now in Cloud Backup or on the Backup tab. Changes a clinician makes reach your phone after your next change or Sync Now.
  • Your sign-in renews itself when the app syncs, for up to 180 days from when you signed in or last changed your password. If the app goes more than about three weeks without syncing, it can end sooner. It also ends if you reset your password, change it on another device, or sign out anywhere else, including the clinician portal. Then Home shows "Sign in again to keep backing up". Everything on your phone is still there. Tap the message and sign in again.
  • Signing out ends your sign-in on all your devices. The data on your phone stays.
  • Changing your password (in Cloud Backup, under Profile) keeps you signed in on that phone and signs you out on your other devices.
  • Our service can read Cloud Backup data; it isn't end-to-end encrypted. See our Privacy Policy.

If you see "Please update Next Step to the latest version to keep using Cloud Backup", update the app. If you see "Next Step is briefly down for maintenance", try again later.

Moving to a new phone

With Cloud Backup: install Next Step, open Settings, then Cloud Backup, then I already have an account. Sign in and tap Sync Now.

Without Cloud Backup: restore an encrypted backup file you exported (see Backup files). Photos aren't inside backup files, and the photo ZIP can't be loaded back into the app, so without Cloud Backup your photos can't be moved into Next Step on a new phone.

Your meal and snack times and your check-in questions come back from Cloud Backup on a new phone. That includes changes you made to the app's starting meal and snack times, if you sign in on the new phone before you change its meal schedule. A starting time you deleted comes back in Recently Deleted. Changes made before our October 2026 update reach Cloud Backup once the phone you made them on syncs with the updated app. So if you changed the starting times before then, update Next Step on your old phone and tap Sync Now there before you sign in on the new phone. Which days a time repeats on, and its nourishment bridge setting, aren't kept in Cloud Backup (backup files include them), so check your meal schedule afterwards in Settings, under Meal schedule. Affirmations you save to a meal in the Affirmations game aren't kept in Cloud Backup either, but backup files include them.

Known issues: a starting time that has been permanently removed (with Delete Forever or Empty Trash, or about 30 days after you deleted it) comes back on a new phone as the app first set it up, and changes to it there may not be backed up. Delete it again if you don't need it. If you use Next Step on more than one phone, changing or deleting a meal or snack time on one phone doesn't change it on another phone that already has it. That phone keeps its old version, and can put it back in Cloud Backup the next time its meal schedule changes, so make the same change on each phone. Some check-ins backed up before our October 2026 update show on a new phone under their meal type, not the name of their meal or snack time. A very large first sync can fail; if it does, tap Sync Now again, and email us if it keeps happening.

"We couldn't open your recovery data"

This screen means the app couldn't open its data on your phone. Your data hasn't been deleted.

  1. Tap Try again first. It doesn't change anything, so it's always safe.
  2. If the screen says the app is older than your saved data, update Next Step.
  3. If it says an update couldn't finish preparing your data, check for a newer version of Next Step.
  4. If it still won't open, please email us before tapping Restore the copy from before this update.

That button only appears when an update fails while the app is opening. It shows when the copy was saved (just before the app last tried the update) and asks you to confirm. Restoring puts that copy back, so anything saved on this phone after that time is removed. If the app still can't open afterwards, tap Undo the restore to put your data back as it was. If you use Cloud Backup, your next sync brings back what had already been backed up. If you have a backup file that's newer than that copy, importing it after a restore also brings the newer items back. If crash reporting is off, we only find out about this problem if you tell us.

Check-in reminders

Turn reminders on in Settings, under Check-in Reminders. They're scheduled on your phone, nothing is sent from a server, and they're off until you turn them on. By default a reminder only says "A check-in is ready when you are."; showing the check-in's name is your choice.

If a reminder doesn't arrive, check that:

  • reminders are switched on, and Next Step is allowed to send notifications (if it isn't, the reminder settings show a button to fix that);
  • that check-in is on in "Remind me for" and in your meal schedule, and has a valid time;
  • on Android, Next Step is allowed "Alarms & reminders" (the app offers Allow on-time reminders). Without it, a reminder can arrive late, occasionally by up to an hour;
  • your phone's Do Not Disturb or Focus settings aren't holding it back.

The app schedules up to 60 reminders at a time and tells you if some were left out. Erasing the app's data cancels all reminders.

Backup files

To export: open the Backup tab, go to Manual Backup (Advanced), enter a passphrase of at least 6 characters, tap Export Encrypted Backup, then Download Backup File and save it somewhere safe. The file is encrypted on your phone. We never see your passphrase and can't recover it: if you lose it, the file can't be opened.

To restore: tap Upload Backup File (or paste the backup text), enter the passphrase and tap Decrypt & Restore. Restoring merges the file with what's on your phone, keeping the newest version of each item. It doesn't remove anything you added or changed. If one of the app's starting meal or snack times is still as the app set it up, the file's version replaces it, or moves it to the app's trash (Recently Deleted) if it was deleted in the file. If a clinician's check-in questions, or questions you changed more recently, are in use, the file's questions are added switched off.

Backup files don't include photos. To save those, use Download All Photos (ZIP) on the Backup tab; the ZIP isn't encrypted. Restoring a file keeps your crash-reporting choice.

Connecting with a clinician

You need a Cloud Backup account. Then, in the Care Team tab, either:

  • enter or scan your clinician's connection code, and they approve the request; or
  • share your Personal Code so your clinician can send you a request, and approve it under "Wants to connect with you".

You choose what each clinician can see. Meals & Photos is set to View by default; Challenges, Goals and Templates are set to None. Only you can change these. To change the defaults for new connections, open Settings, then Clinician Access Defaults.

Revoke Access in Care Team removes a clinician's access straight away. It also hides your message history with them, for both of you. Reconnecting needs their approval again. If you reconnect, your earlier messages and the access you'd set for them come back; the defaults for new connections don't apply.

Messages don't send notifications: new ones appear when the app or portal is open. Please don't use messages in an emergency.

We don't check clinicians' identities, licences or credentials, so only connect with someone you know.

Clinicians: create your account in the Next Step app, then sign in to the clinician portal with the same email address and password.

A concern about a clinician or a message

You can revoke a clinician's access at any time in Care Team. They lose access to your data straight away. Then email us at support@cipher.haus with "Concern" in the subject line and tell us what happened. We read every report, and we may close accounts that break our Terms of Service.

Deleting your account or your data

You can delete your Cloud Backup account in the app, or by email if you can't use the app. See Delete your account for the steps, and for what's deleted and what's kept. To erase what's on your phone, open Settings, go to Data and tap Erase All Data on This Device. Erasing puts your settings back to their defaults, but keeps your crash-reporting choice.

Privacy and data requests

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision we've made. Email support@cipher.haus from the address on your account; you don't need an account to ask. We'll check the request came from you and respond within 45 days. See our Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Reporting a security issue

Please email security@cipher.haus rather than support. Our security.txt has the details.
