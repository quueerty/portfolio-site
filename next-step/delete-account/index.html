Delete your Next Step account

Last updated: October 5, 2026

You can delete your Next Step Cloud Backup account at any time: in the app, or by email if you can't use the app. Deleting it is permanent and can't be undone.

If you never created an account, we don't hold an account or recovery data for you. Your data is only on your phone; see Erase the data on your phone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Next Step and tap the gear button on Home to open Settings.
  2. Tap Cloud Backup, then Profile. (You need to be signed in.)
  3. Under Delete Account, tap the Delete Account button.
  4. Enter your password, then tap Yes, Permanently Delete My Account.

The app tells you when your account has been deleted, and signs you out of Cloud Backup on that phone. Forgotten your password? We only send a reset link to a confirmed email address. If Profile asks you to confirm your email, do that there before you sign out: enter the code from our email, or, if you don't have one, choose Send a new code and enter the code from that email (see Confirming your email address). Then, in Cloud Backup, tap Sign Out (if your email still isn't confirmed, the app warns you first and offers Confirm Email), then I already have an account. If Cloud Backup says "Sign in again to keep backing up", the sign-in form is already open. Type your email address and tap Forgot password?. After resetting it, sign in again and then delete your account. Or ask us by email instead, whether or not your email address is confirmed.

Before you delete

  • Want to keep your data? Export a backup file and your photos from the Backup tab first. Some data, like your messages, can't be exported in the app; email us if you'd like a copy before you delete. We can only send a copy to an email address you've confirmed (see Confirming your email address), so confirm yours first if Profile asks you to.
  • Your conversations are deleted for the other person too. Deleting your account deletes your whole message history with each clinician, including their messages, and they aren't told. If they might want a record, let them know first.
  • Clinicians: if you're the only owner of a practice that still has other members, make another member an owner first; until then, deletion is refused. A practice with no other members is deleted with your account.
  • If the app can't confirm that your photos were removed from cloud storage, it keeps your account and asks you to try again. If it says deletion isn't confirmed yet, try again a little later.

Ask us by email

Email support@cipher.haus from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Next Step account". Include your username if you know it. We'll reply to that address to check the request really came from you. Nothing is deleted until you've confirmed from it. Then we'll delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it's done. It's deleted in exactly the same way as in the app. You don't need the app to ask. This works whether or not your account's email address was ever confirmed.

If you can no longer use that email address, write from another address and tell us your username. We'll need to be confident the account is yours before we delete anything, so we'll ask for details only the account holder would know, and we may not be able to act if we can't confirm it.

What's deleted

  • Your account: name, username, email address, password, two-factor settings and sign-ins.
  • Everything synced from your phone: check-ins with their answers and reflections, Nourishment Bridge check-ins, your meal and snack schedule, Challenge Ladder steps, recovery goals, check-in questions, settings, and anything in the trash.
  • All your photos in cloud storage, including photos you removed in the app earlier. A second clean-up runs about six minutes later, to catch any upload that was still in progress. If our server restarts first, or the clean-up fails, it's normally tried again until it succeeds.
  • Your message threads and meal comments with clinicians, for both of you, and their reactions.
  • Your connections with clinicians.
  • The record of clinician access to your data.
  • Practice chat messages you wrote, or that were about you.
  • If you're a clinician: your clinician profile and connection code, your practice memberships, your template library, and any practice you were the only member of.

What's kept

  • Data on your phone. Deleting your account doesn't touch it; see below.
  • Database backups made by our hosting provider, Render. Deleted data can remain in any backup made before you deleted it, until that backup expires.
  • Emails we sent you (email confirmation and password reset) stay in your inbox. Our email provider, Resend, keeps its own records of them under its retention settings.
  • Crash reports and performance samples already sent to Sentry, until Sentry deletes them. They don't include your name or email address, but some performance samples, and occasionally a report about a failed photo upload, contain your account ID.
  • Our server logs, which don't include account IDs, IP addresses or what you sent.
  • Copies of photos already opened in the clinician portal can stay in that web browser's cache.
  • If you're a clinician: records that you opened a client's data stay in that client's access record, without your identity. Check-in question sets you sent to clients stay with them, and items you added or changed in a client's account with Edit access stay in their account.

Erase the data on your phone

Your phone keeps its own copy of your data, with or without an account. To erase it:

  1. Open Settings and scroll down to Data.
  2. Tap Erase All Data on This Device.
  3. Type ERASE, then tap Erase Everything.

This deletes the app's database (including its automatic copies), the photos saved in the app, your settings and your scheduled reminders, and signs you out of Cloud Backup on this phone. It doesn't delete your Cloud Backup account; use the steps above for that. Your settings go back to their defaults, except that your crash-reporting choice is kept. On iPhone, the app's data stays out of iCloud backups.

It doesn't clear the app's temporary cache, where copies of exported backup files and some photos can stay until your phone clears them. To remove those too, uninstall the app. Files you've saved or shared somewhere else aren't affected.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can delete check-ins, schedule items, goals and Challenge Ladder steps in the app. They stay in the app's trash for 30 days and are then permanently removed from your phone. On our server, the deletion arrives the next time your phone syncs. Once 30 days have passed since you deleted them, they're permanently removed the next time your phone sends a change to our server. If that doesn't happen first, an hourly clean-up removes them about 32 days after you deleted them.

Once the change syncs, we stop giving out links to a photo you remove, or to one on a check-in you delete, and the photo is deleted from cloud storage about a day later. A deleted check-in's photos go at the latest when the check-in is permanently removed. A few photos stay until you delete your account. These are photos you removed before our October 2026 update, and photos on check-ins permanently removed before then. A photo removed from a Nourishment Bridge check-in on its own also stays. Rarely, so does a photo removed before its upload finished, or on a phone whose clock is behind. If you'd like those deleted without deleting your account, email us from your account's address with "Delete removed photos" in the subject line.
