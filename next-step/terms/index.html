Next Step isn't medical care or an emergency service

Next Step is a support tool for people in eating disorder recovery. It isn't medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor, therapist, dietitian or treatment team. If something in the app doesn't match your care team's guidance, follow your care team, and talk with a doctor or your care team before changing anything about your care.

What Next Step does

Next Step offers meal and snack check-ins with a pacing timer, grounding and breathing tools, coping skills, an Affirmations Book, crisis helplines, recovery goals, a challenge ladder and reminders. These work on your phone, offline. With an optional Cloud Backup account you can also back up and sync your data, connect with clinicians and message them. Clinicians create a separate clinician account in the clinician portal; clinician accounts, and System Console accounts, can't sign in to the app. We may change, add or remove features over time.

Cloud Backup

Cloud Backup is optional and free. After you sign in on a phone, the app syncs when you make changes and when you tap Sync Now. Its first sync downloads your account, merges it with what's on the phone and uploads the result. Your sign-in renews itself as the app syncs, for up to 180 days from when you signed in or last changed your password. If it ends, backup stops until you sign in again, and the app tells you. Check "Last synced" in Cloud Backup from time to time. We can't promise that every change is backed up, so for anything you can't lose, keep an encrypted backup file and a photo ZIP as well (Settings, Backup & Restore, Manual Backup). Backup files don't include photos, and the photo ZIP isn't encrypted. How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Paid plans for clinicians

Clients never pay to use Next Step. Clinicians can use the clinician portal for free, connected with 1 client, or subscribe to a paid plan. A clinician's own plan covers the clients they see on their own. A practice's plan covers the clients its clinicians see through the practice, and its limit is shared by all of them. If the practice has no paid plan, or a clinician leaves it, those clients count toward the clinician's own plan:

  • Starter: up to 5 clients.
  • Basic: up to 15 clients.
  • Growth: up to 30 clients.
  • Professional: up to 65 clients.
  • Clinic (practices only): up to 120 clients.

A practice that needs more than 120 clients can contact us for a custom plan, which is agreed and invoiced separately. A practice's owner can set a lower limit for each clinician in the practice.

A plan changes only how many clients a clinician can connect with. Everything else in the portal is the same on every plan. A limit stops a new connection, and stops a client who is already connected being moved into a list that's full (a practice's or your own). Nothing already connected is removed: if a plan ends or a clinician is over a limit, clients already connected stay connected. No one is limited before January 1, 2027.

Prices and payment. Prices are listed on our Pricing page and shown on the payment page before you subscribe. Prices are in US dollars and include any applicable taxes. Payments are processed by Stripe, under Stripe's terms. Subscriptions renew automatically each month or year, as you chose, until cancelled. Before you pay, you confirm that you agree to these Terms and that the plan renews automatically. Stripe emails you a reminder about a week before each renewal.

Changing plans. You can switch plans, or between monthly and yearly billing, in "Manage billing" on the portal's Billing page. The change takes effect straight away. Switching to another plan with the same billing period (monthly to monthly, or yearly to yearly): if the new plan costs more for the rest of the current period, the difference for that time is charged at once; if it costs less, the difference is kept as a credit toward your next payments. Switching between monthly and yearly starts a new billing period that day: you're charged the new price at once, less a credit for the unused part of your current period.

Practice prices. A practice pays one price for its plan, however many clinicians it has. Clinicians join a practice only when an owner approves their request, or when we add them at the practice's request.

Suspended accounts. If we suspend an account that pays for a plan, the plan is set to end at the close of the current billing period, and renewed if the suspension ends before then. Meanwhile another owner of the practice can update its payment method and see its invoices on Stripe's page, but can't switch or cancel the plan.

Failed payments. If a payment fails, Stripe tries again over the following days, and the plan stays in place meanwhile. If every attempt fails, the plan ends.

Cancelling. You can cancel at any time from "Manage billing" on the portal's Billing page. Your plan continues until the end of the period you've paid for and isn't renewed. A cancelled plan can't be resumed on Stripe's page; once it has ended, you can subscribe again. When you ask to delete your clinician account, your subscription, and any practice subscription you pay for, stops renewing and you aren't charged during the 30 days before the account is deleted; when it's deleted, they're cancelled. The practice being deleted (for example, when its last member leaves) cancels its subscription immediately. A practice's subscription is managed by the clinician who set it up, and it ends immediately if they leave the practice or are removed from it, and when their account is deleted, so nobody keeps paying for a practice they're no longer part of; another owner can then set it up again.

Refunds. Payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise or where we charged you by mistake (for example, twice). This includes a plan that ends early because it was cancelled before the end of its period (for example, when your account is deleted). Any credit left from a plan change isn't paid out. It stays with your billing record at Stripe and is used toward future payments if you subscribe again, and it's lost if that record is deleted (for example, when your account is deleted). If you think you were charged by mistake, email support@cipher.haus.

Price changes. We'll email subscribers, at their account's email address, at least 30 days before a price change takes effect. You can cancel before then.
