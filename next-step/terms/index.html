Next Step isn't medical care or an emergency service

Next Step is a support tool for people in eating disorder recovery. It isn't medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor, therapist, dietitian or treatment team. The app's prompts and suggestions are general, not medical advice made for you. If something in the app doesn't match your care team's guidance, follow your care team, and talk with a doctor or your care team before changing anything about your care.

Next Step isn't an emergency service. If you're in danger or need urgent medical help, call 911 in the US, or your local emergency number. The crisis lines in the app and on our Support page are there to listen and support you; for a medical emergency, call your emergency number. The app's Call and Text buttons only work on a device that can make calls and send texts.

Messages to clinicians and emails to us aren't watched around the clock, and the app doesn't alert anyone when you send one. Please don't rely on them in an emergency.

Who can use Next Step

You can use the app on your phone without an account. To create a Cloud Backup account, you must be 13 or older. The sign-up screen asks you to confirm this; we don't verify it. We delete accounts we learn belong to children under 13. If you're under 18, we encourage you to use Next Step with the support of a parent, guardian or your treatment team.

What Next Step does

Next Step offers meal and snack check-ins with a pacing timer, grounding and breathing tools, crisis helplines, recovery goals, a challenge ladder and reminders. These work on your phone, offline. With an optional Cloud Backup account you can also back up and sync your data, connect with clinicians and message them. Clinicians use the same account to sign in to the clinician portal. We may change, add or remove features over time.

Your account

  • Give accurate details when you sign up, and keep your password to yourself.
  • Two-factor authentication is optional. If you turn it on, keep the setup key somewhere safe: there's currently no built-in way back into your account if you lose your authenticator.
  • If you think someone else has your password, change it straight away. Changing it signs you out on every device. Then let us know at support@cipher.haus.

Cloud Backup

Cloud Backup is optional and free. When you sign in on a phone, the app uploads what's on it, then syncs when you make changes and when you tap Sync Now. A sign-in lasts 30 days; after that, or after a password change, backup stops until you sign in again. Check "Last synced" in Cloud Backup from time to time. We can't promise that every change is backed up, so for anything you can't lose, keep an encrypted backup file as well (Backup tab, Manual Backup). How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Clinicians and care teams

  • Any care relationship is between you and your clinician. Cipher Haus isn't part of it and doesn't provide care.
  • We don't check clinicians' identities, licences or credentials. Only connect with someone you know and trust.
  • You decide what each clinician can see, area by area, and only you can change it. Your meal and snack check-ins are shared by default when you connect. You can revoke a clinician's access at any time in the Care Team tab.
  • We don't monitor your messages, and there are no notifications for new ones: they appear when the app or portal is open.
  • Sharing something with a clinician doesn't mean they'll see it straight away. Clinicians don't get notifications about new check-ins or messages; they only see them when they open the app or portal. Agree with your clinician how and when they'll look at what you share.
  • If you or a clinician deletes an account, your message history with each other is deleted for both of you, and the other person isn't told. Email us if you'd like a copy of your messages first.
  • Next Step isn't a medical record or a record of care.

If you're a clinician: you're responsible for your own professional obligations, including whether and how you use Next Step with clients. Don't rely on Next Step for urgent contact or as a clinical record. If you delete your account, your message threads with clients are deleted for both of you, while changes you made in a client's account with Edit access, and check-in question sets you sent them, stay with the client.
