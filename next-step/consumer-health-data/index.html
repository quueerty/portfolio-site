Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Next Step app, its optional Cloud Backup account and its clinician portal, operated by Cipher Haus ("we", "us"). It applies to everyone who uses Next Step, including people in Washington, Nevada and Connecticut. Because Next Step is an eating disorder recovery app, we treat the personal data it handles as consumer health data, including data that could show that you use it.

Contact: support@cipher.haus.

Consumer health data we collect, and why

If you use Next Step without an account, your recovery data stays on your phone and we don't receive it. We only receive crash reports (see below), unless you turn them off. If you create a Cloud Backup account, we collect:

  • Check-ins: when they happened and how long they took, how each one went, your answers to check-in questions, and anything you write.
  • Photos you choose to add to a check-in.
  • Your recovery plan in the app: your meal and snack schedule, Nourishment Bridge check-ins, Challenge Ladder steps (including the names of support people you add), recovery goals, your check-in questions and your settings.
  • Messages and meal comments between you and clinicians you connect with, and practice chat about you.
  • Your care connections: which clinicians you're connected with, what each one can see, and a record of when they opened your data.
  • Account details (name, username and email address), because they can show that you use a recovery app.
  • Crash reports and performance samples: your phone model, operating system, app version, device details, a short trail of what happened just before a problem, a random ID for the installation, and the IP address a report comes from. Some performance samples may include web addresses that contain your account ID.

We use this data only to provide the features you ask for: to store, back up and sync your data, restore it on another phone, show it to clinicians you choose, deliver your messages, run your account (including email-confirmation and password-reset emails), keep the service secure, and find and fix problems.

Where it comes from

  • You, when you use the app or the clinician portal.
  • Your phone, automatically: crash reports and device details.
  • Clinicians you connect with: their messages and comments, check-in question sets they send you, and changes they make in your account if you give them Edit access.

How it's processed

Your data is stored on your phone. If you use Cloud Backup, it's sent over HTTPS to our server and stored there in a form our server can read; it isn't end-to-end encrypted. Our service providers process it for us: Render (our server and database), Cloudflare (photo storage), Resend (account emails) and Sentry (crash reports). On Android, the app includes Google's ML Kit, which it uses only to scan a clinician's QR code. The scan happens on your phone; Google says ML Kit sends it device and app information, an installation ID and performance measurements.

Who we share it with

  • Clinicians you connect with, so they can support your care, and only the areas you allow: your meal and snack check-ins, Nourishment Bridge check-ins and their photos (shared by default when you connect, and you can turn it off); your Challenge Ladder steps, goals and check-in questions (only if you allow it); your account name; and your messages and meal comments with them. If your clinicians belong to a practice together, clinicians in that practice whom you've approved can see the practice chat about you.
  • Google, on Android, the device and app information described above, from the ML Kit component the QR scanner uses.
  • Courts, regulators or law enforcement, only where the law requires us to.

We don't share consumer health data with any affiliates. We don't sell consumer health data.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • get access to it, including a list of the third parties we've shared it with and how to contact them;
  • have it deleted;
  • withdraw consent, and have us stop collecting or sharing it;
  • appeal if we turn down your request.

You can also act yourself in the app: change or revoke a clinician's access in Care Team, turn off crash reporting in Settings, under Privacy & Diagnostics, delete your account in Cloud Backup, under Profile, and erase the data on your phone in Settings, under Data. See Delete your account for what's deleted and what's kept.

You can review and change most of your data in the app. To ask us to correct anything you can't change yourself, email us.

How to make a request

Email support@cipher.haus with "Health data request" in the subject line, and tell us what you'd like us to do. If you have an account, write from the email address on it: we'll reply to that address to confirm the request came from you before we act. You don't need to create an account to make a request. If you don't have one, we don't hold your recovery data; crash reports aren't linked to a name or email address, so we may not be able to find them.

We'll respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need longer, we'll tell you why within those 45 days, and take up to 45 more. If you ask us to delete your account and its data, we'll do it within 30 days of confirming the request came from you; see Delete your account for what's kept. Requests are free.

Appeals

If we turn down your request, we'll tell you why. To appeal, reply with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll answer in writing within 45 days, explaining what we decided and why. If we still turn it down, you can contact your attorney general:

  • Washington Attorney General complaint form
  • Nevada Attorney General consumer complaints
  • Connecticut Attorney General complaint form

Tracking and location

No third party collects data about your activity over time and across other websites or apps through Next Step. Next Step doesn't use your location, and we don't use geofencing.

Changes and effective date

This policy takes effect on October 2, 2026. If we change it, we'll publish the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. We won't apply a material change, such as a new use of data we already hold or a new kind of recipient, to data we already hold without your consent.
