gar.page

Strip tracking from links and make short, private links.

Links carry more than an address: utm tags, click IDs, codes that tie a shared link back to the person who shared it, and redirect hops that log every visit. Share a link to gar.page and the tracking comes off on your phone. You get a clean short link, copied and ready to send on.

  • Clean only removes tracking on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Clean & shorten makes a short gar.page link from the clean address, and follows short links like bit.ly and t.co to the real page first.
  • Where does it go? shows where a short or tracking link really leads, and every hop on the way, without your phone visiting any of them.
  • Self-destructing links: after one open, or after an hour, a day, a week or a month. You can also delete a link for everyone at any time.
  • QR codes: show any link as a QR code, or share a picture of one to read the link inside it before you open it.
  • Encrypted backups of your history, protected with your passphrase.

Free, with no account. The server keeps only the clean destination and the date: no IP addresses, no device details, no record of who made a link, no click counts, no logs. Your history stays on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Android and iPhone apps are coming soon.
