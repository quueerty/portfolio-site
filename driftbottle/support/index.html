Driftbottle Support

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Need help with Driftbottle? Email us at support@cipher.haus. We read every message.

Contact us

Write to support@cipher.haus about:

  • trouble signing in, with your password, or with two-factor authentication;
  • a bug or crash — tell us your phone model, and what you were doing when it happened;
  • a bottle or a player you're worried about (reporting it in the app is quickest — see below);
  • privacy questions, and requests for a copy of your data, a correction, or deleting your account;
  • child safety — put "Child safety" in the subject (see our Child Safety Standards).

If it's about your account, email us from the address you signed up with, and we'll reply to that address to check it's really you before changing anything. We'll never ask for your password or your two-factor codes — don't send them.

Reports of harmful content come first: we aim to act on them within 24 hours. Data requests are answered within 30 days, and everything else as soon as we can.

Report a bottle or a label

Open the Bottles tab, then Found (or Saved), and tap the flag button on the bottle. Pick a reason, add details if you like, and tap Submit report. Reports go to our moderators, never to the person you reported.

A label someone else drew can be reported the same way, with the flag button where it's shown: on a bottle of yours in Bottles › Dropped, or on a labeled bottle in your inventory on the Drop tab.

Block or unblock someone

To block the bottler who dropped a bottle you found, tap the block button on the bottle in Bottles › Found and confirm. Neither of you will find the other's bottles after that. To block someone who drew a label — on a bottle of yours, a bottle you found, or a labeled bottle in your inventory — use the block button next to that label. They won't find your bottles after that, and you won't see that label again; you may still come across their bottles, because we never tell you who drew a label, and hiding their bottles would. To unblock someone, tap the account icon at the top right, then Blocked Bottlers, and tap Unblock.

Forgotten your password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email and tap Send Reset Link. Open the link in the email on your phone within 30 minutes to choose a new password. You can also sign in with the Magic Link tab, which emails you a one-time sign-in link.

Lost your two-factor authenticator

When the app asks for your two-factor code, tap Lost your authenticator? Use a recovery code and enter one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned two-factor on. Each code works once. Once you're signed in, you can get a fresh set under the account icon › Account › Two-Factor Authentication › Get New Recovery Codes.

Lost your recovery codes too? Email us from your account's email address. Once we've confirmed the account is yours, we can turn two-factor authentication off: every device is signed out, and we email your address to let you know.

Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app, or by email if you can't use the app. See Delete your Driftbottle account for the steps, and for what's deleted and what's kept.
