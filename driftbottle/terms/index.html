What Driftbottle is

Driftbottle lets you drop virtual "message bottles" — a voice recording, photo, or text message — at a real place, for other users who go there to find. Bottle sizes and daily drop bundles are earned with in-app coins, a virtual currency usable only within the app. Coins are earned through gameplay. Coin purchases with real money may be offered in the future through Apple's or Google's in-app purchase system; if they are, those purchases will be processed and billed entirely by Apple or Google — we never see or store your payment details — and subject to their respective refund policies rather than ours. Coins, however obtained, have no real-world monetary value and can't be redeemed for cash, exchanged, or transferred outside the app.

Your location — please read

Driftbottle uses your device's precise location to show you the bottles you can pick up where you are, and to leave a bottle near where you are when you drop one. We don't store your location. The only places we keep are where bottles are, and a bottle you drop (or leave with "Drop here") isn't kept at your exact spot, but at an approximate one within 40 metres of it (see our Privacy Policy). Other players never see coordinates, a map, how far away anything is, or when a bottle was dropped, but finding a bottle means being close to it (within its pickup radius of 50 to 100 metres), so:

  • whoever picks up a bottle you drop sees your bottler name (or username), and can tell you were somewhere near there at some point;
  • when you pick up a bottle, its owner only sees that it was found — not who found it or where. The app doesn't show them when, though if they check often they might notice roughly when.

Don't drop bottles somewhere that reveals where you live, go to school or spend your time unless you're comfortable with strangers knowing, and don't drop or look for bottles in places that aren't safe or appropriate to visit (private property, restricted areas, hazardous locations). Use good judgment, and never meet up with another user in person based on bottle activity without normal in-person-meeting safety precautions — we have no way to verify who's on the other end of a bottle. If you're under 18, don't meet up with anyone you know only from Driftbottle.
