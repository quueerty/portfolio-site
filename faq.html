Help

What is Cipher Haus?

A small workshop building two quiet, independent apps — see the home page for what they're for.

How do I contact support?

Email support@cipher.haus and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

How do I delete my account or data?

Both apps let you delete your account directly from within the app, in account settings. If that doesn't work for you for any reason, email support@cipher.haus and we'll take care of it. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. We don't sell data or share it with advertisers, and neither app uses advertising or analytics trackers. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

I found a security issue — how do I report it?

Email security@cipher.haus directly rather than filing a public report. See security.txt for details.

Something's not working — what should I include when I write in?

It helps to include which app you're using, roughly when the issue happened, and what you expected to happen instead. Screens or screenshots help too, if you're comfortable sharing them.
