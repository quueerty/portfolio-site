Shape & Co. Support

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Need help with Shape & Co.? Email us at support@cipher.haus. We read every message.

Contact us

Write to support@cipher.haus about:

  • a bug, or progress that didn't save: tell us your phone and what you were doing when it happened;
  • a listing on the Labs or theme market that breaks the rules below;
  • privacy questions, or deleting your data.

If it's about your save, include your invite code (open Invite in the game to see it). It's how we find your save, since the game never asks for your name or email.

Your save

You play as a guest: there's no account or sign-in. Your progress is saved on your phone and backed up to our servers automatically. Android's own backup brings it back with the rest of your phone's data on a new device. A game you play with a theme's own rules is kept on your phone only.

Market rules

On the Labs market you can sell designs from the Workshop, and on the theme market, themes. Everything is bought and sold with coins earned in the game, never with money. When you list something:

  • Names and shop names can't hold rude words, links, @ signs or long numbers. Don't put your real name, or anything about yourself or other people, in them.
  • Only use pictures you made or are allowed to use. Pictures of real people, yourself included, or of anything personal or unkind aren't allowed.
  • Themes that use a video can't be sold; videos stay on your phone.
  • We look at every theme before it goes on sale, which can take a few days, and tell you if we turn it down.
  • Something you mark open source stays open source: anyone can look inside it, and buyers can change a copy and sell it as a remix that names yours.

We remove listings that break these rules.

Reporting a listing

Open the listing and tap Report, then say what's wrong with it. That sends it to us and hides that seller for you. Hide this seller hides them without a report. You can also email support@cipher.haus. There's no chat in the game, and no way for other players to contact you.

Deleting your data

Email support@cipher.haus with your invite code (open Invite in the game to see it). We'll delete your save, your codes and your market listings, with a theme's pictures, from our servers within 30 days and confirm by email.

Uninstalling the game removes everything on your phone, including your themes, but not the copy of your save on our servers. Designs and themes other players already bought stay on their phones. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
