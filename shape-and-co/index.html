Shape & Co.

Train every station, build a shape factory and grow it into a company.

Shape & Co. is a factory game where every station is a small skill game. Gather shapes, inspect them, prep and process them, then turn them into wheels, gears and frames, and assemble those into products to sell. How well you play each station sets how good and how fast it runs.

  • Train your stations. Each has its own quick game, scored on quality and speed, and runs better while you're away the better you train it.
  • Grow the line. Construct new stations from what the last one makes, unlock the ore line, build components and assemble products, then research automation to merge whole lines into factories, Suppliers and a Company.
  • Robots that sell, service, carry, speed up and train your stations, plus a town, a lab, daily goals, achievements and contracts.
  • The Workshop: make wire, dyes, switches and LEDs, wire them into logic gates and circuits, or dye fabric into clothes, and sell your designs on the Labs market.
  • Themes change the whole look: the map, every station's picture, the names and the icons. Run your line as a bistro, a farm, an office, a mountain camp, a neon city, a space station or a pirate ship, or make your own and sell it on the theme market.

Your line keeps working while the game is closed, and your progress is saved to the cloud automatically. No account or sign-in, no ads, and no in-app purchases: everything costs coins earned in the game. For players aged 13 and over.

Android and iPhone apps are coming soon.
