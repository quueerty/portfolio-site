Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Shape & Co. is a game made by Cipher Haus, LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@cipher.haus.

The short version

  • You play as a guest. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is saved on our servers so it isn't lost.
  • If you sell a design on the Labs market, other players see it with the name and shop name you gave it.
  • Pictures you draw or import for a theme stay on your device. They're sent to us only if you put that theme up for sale on the theme market, where other players see them. Videos never leave your device.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

What we collect and why

A guest ID. When the game first starts, it signs in to Google Firebase as an anonymous guest. Firebase gives the game a random ID that isn't linked to your name or Google account. We use it only to keep your save separate from everyone else's.

Your game save. Your factory, coins, robots, levels, town and other progress are saved on your device and copied to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your guest ID, so your progress is backed up.

Promo codes and invite codes. If you redeem a promo code, we record that your guest ID used it, so each code can only be used once per player. If you open Invite in the game, you get an invite code linked to your guest ID. If a friend joins with your code, we record that their guest ID used it and whether they've started playing, so the game can give you both your rewards. Neither of you can see who the other is.

The Labs market. If you list a design you made in the Workshop, we store the design, its name, the shop name you chose, its price and how many have sold. Other players see the name, shop name, picture, price and what it does; they can see how it's made only if you mark it open source, or after buying it. If you buy a design, we record that your guest ID bought it, so you get it once and the seller gets the coins. If you report a listing, we store the listing, the reason and your guest ID so we can look into it. Don't put your real name or anything about yourself or other people in names: we may remove listings that do.

Themes. A theme you make (its pictures, names and rules) is kept on your device only, and so is a game you play with a theme's own rules. To add a picture or a short video, you pick it yourself: the game can't look through your photos or files.

The theme market. If you put a theme up for sale, we store the theme, its name, your shop name, its price and how many have sold, and its pictures are uploaded to Google Firebase (Cloud Storage). A theme that uses a video can't be put up for sale, so videos are never uploaded. We look at every theme before it appears on the market. After that, other players see its name, your shop name, a preview picture and a short summary, and anyone who buys it gets the whole theme, pictures included, which is then kept on their device. If you mark a theme open source, any player can look inside it, and a buyer can change a copy and sell that copy, still using your pictures, as a remix that names your theme. A theme can be made open source later but not closed again.

A still picture you import is redrawn at the size the game needs, so the original file, and details stored in it such as where a photo was taken, never leave your device. Animated pictures (GIF, WebP, APNG) are uploaded as they are, with any details stored in the file. Uploaded pictures can't be changed or removed in the game: ask us (see below). Don't use pictures of yourself or other real people, or anything personal: we don't approve those, and we remove them if we find them.

If you buy a theme, we record that your guest ID bought it. If you report a theme, we store the same things as for a design.

Technical information. When the game talks to Firebase, Google receives your device's IP address and basic information about the request, as any online service does. Google uses this to run and protect the service. We don't use it to identify you.

We don't collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, advertising ID or device identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. The only photos we ever receive are pictures you chose for a theme and then put up for sale.

Who processes your data

Data is handled for us by these service providers, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage): guest sign-in, saves, market listings and the pictures of themes on the market. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Play and the App Store: downloads, under Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Children

Shape & Co. is meant for players aged 13 and over, because the markets show names other players type and pictures other players made. We never ask for a name, email address, phone number or location, from anyone, and nobody has to add a picture: importing one into a theme is a choice. There's no chat or way for players to contact each other. Names are filtered, every theme is checked by us before it's shown, any listing can be reported, and we take down listings that break these rules.

If you believe a child under 13 has listed something or sent us personal information, contact support@cipher.haus and we'll delete it. Parents and guardians can ask us to delete a save at any time (see below).

Keeping and deleting your data

Your save stays on our servers so you can keep playing. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device, but not the copy of your save on our servers.

To have your save, codes and market listings (with a theme's pictures) deleted from our servers, email support@cipher.haus with your invite code (open Invite in the game to see it). We'll delete the data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. Copies of a design or theme that other players already bought stay on their devices, and a remix of an open-source theme may keep using its pictures unless you ask us to remove those too. Your email to us is kept only as long as we need to answer it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each guest read and change only their own save. On the market, the rules let players read listings, and let only the seller, buyers (or anyone, if it's open source) read a design or a theme. A theme's uploaded pictures can be loaded by anyone playing the game, and nobody can replace them after we've checked them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters (for example, the game starts collecting something new), we'll also say so in the game or on the store page.
